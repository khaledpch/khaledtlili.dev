## Build once

Produce a traceable artifact and promote it between environments rather than rebuilding independently at each stage.

## Design for failure

Use health checks, explicit rollback mechanisms, and observable deployment boundaries. Keep secrets out of logs.

## Takeaways

Optimize for reproducibility and recovery.
